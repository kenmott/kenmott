# Ken Mott

I build AI products that people actually use.

I've spent 15+ years in product and program management. Today I also build, using Claude Code and local models, because the fastest way to learn what AI can really do is to ship something with it. MBA and BS in Electrical Engineering, Seattle University.

**The question I work on:** what does it take to get AI out of the demo and into something a real person relies on every day?

## What I've shipped

**Ken Mott Planner**
A retirement and financial planner that runs entirely in your browser. Your plan stays on your device, and nothing gets sent anywhere.

**[Handy fork](https://github.com/kenmott/Handy/tree/ken/escape-v0.10.0)**
Local speech to text that runs offline. I wanted my dictation private and on my own machine, so I forked it to fix a problem I kept hitting: pressing Escape mid recording threw away what I said. Now it saves to History instead.

## How I work

- Start with one real user, usually me or someone I know
- Ship a working version before writing the deck
- Keep personal data on the device unless there's a strong reason not to
- Run models locally when it's good enough, and use the cloud when it isn't
